Simulations & Field Reversals

AFT Baseline (2015)

This video demonstrates the evolution of the Sun's magnetic field following the Solar Cycle 24 maximum. AFT assimilates active regions and advects magnetic flux using observed surface currents.

North Pole Reversal (2013)

Solar Cycle 24 northern polar reversal. The video illustrates that the "triple reversal" was not multiple true reversals, but rather a single reversal in late 2013 with lingering poleward flux streams.

South Pole Reversal (2014)

Southern polar reversal mapping. The movie illustrates the persistence and stability of the Sun's active longitudes over the southern polar cap as the HMI data assimilation progresses.

Giant Cell Flow Streams

This movie overlays daily flow streamlines onto radial vorticity maps, showing views from the equator and the North Pole.

The 2017 Solar Eclipse

In Salem, Oregon, Dr. David Hathaway captured the beautiful corona configurations of the August 21, 2017 Total Solar Eclipse. These images were taken using a digital SLR camera with a 400mm lens at f/8, ISO 400.

Short Exposure: Stack of 1/2000s, 1/500s, 1/125s, and 1/30s exposures.

Medium Exposure: Stack including a 1/8s exposure, revealing mid-corona structures.

Long Exposure: Stack up to 1/2s, highlighting the outer coronal streams.

Montage: Compilation of partial phases captured at 1/500s using neutral density filters.
