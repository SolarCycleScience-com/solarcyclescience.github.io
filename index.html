about this site

a window into the life of our star

The Sun provides Earth with warmth, light, and the energy that fuels our entire ecosystem. as the largest body in the solar system, it guides the planets in their orbits and has been worshiped by cultures throughout history — yet there is still much we do not know.

Since the invention of the telescope, knowledge of the Sun has grown at an extraordinary rate. Each discovery brings new ideas and often conflict. Even now, our understanding is constantly being redefined.

This site was built and is maintained by solar physicists Dr. Lisa Upton, Dr. David Hathaway, and Dr. Bibhuti Kumar Jha, supported by grants from NASA and NSF, including a NASA grant to support the COFFIES DRIVE Center.
