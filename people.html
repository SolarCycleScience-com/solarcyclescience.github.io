Heliophysicists

About Us

We are solar physicists dedicated to studying active regions, surface transport currents, and predicting the solar cycle.

Together, we developed the Advective Flux Transport (AFT) model. We created this website to share our data, models, and love of solar science with both the public and our colleagues.

Dr. Lisa Upton

Lead Scientist

Dr. Upton began her solar physics career in 2006 analyzing coronal loops using data from SDO, TRaCE, and Hinode. She earned her Ph.D. in Physics from Vanderbilt University, writing her dissertation on magnetic flux transport and solar cycle modeling, during which she co-developed the AFT model with Dr. David Hathaway.

She has served as a co-chair for the NASA/NOaa Solar Cycle 25 Prediction Panel. Her research goals target solar dynamo dynamics, space climate modeling, and bridging the solar interior with the atmosphere using SFT.

Contact: lisa@solarcyclescience.com

Dr. David Hathaway

NASA Solar Physicist (Retired)

Dr. Hathaway received his Ph.D. in astrophysics from the University of Colorado in 1979. He worked at the National Solar Observatory before moving to NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center, where he served as the head of the Solar Physics Branch from 1996 to 2010. He retired from NASA ames Research Center in 2016.

Over a distinguished career, he has published over 200 papers and received the NASA Exceptional Scientific achievement Medal in 2014 for discovering the Sun's shallow meridional return flow and giant convection cells. He was also awarded the NASA Inventor of the Year award in 2002 for inventing VISaR image registration technology.

Contact: dave.hathaway@comcast.net

Dr. Bibhuti Kumar Jha

Postdoctoral Researcher

Dr. Jha received his Ph.D. in solar astrophysics from the Indian Institute of astrophysics (IIa) in Nainital, India in 2022. He joined the Southwest Research Institute (SwRI) in Boulder in January 2023 as a Postdoctoral Researcher, collaborating with Dr. Lisa Upton on SFT modeling.

His research targets automatic feature extraction (sunspots, filaments, plages) in solar images, machine learning applications in heliophysics, and tracking active regions. He is the developer of autoTaB, an automated bipolar magnetic region (BMR) tracking algorithm.

Contact: bkjha.sun@gmail.com • Website: bibhuraushan.github.io
