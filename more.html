Useful Links & Portals

Expand your search with curated links targeting solar physics institutes, NASA spacecraft missions, and general astronomy websites.

Solar Physics Portals

The Sun Today

Real-time updates, analyses, and news on solar flares, space weather, and auroral alerts.

NASA SDO

The Solar Dynamics Observatory mission portal, capturing continuous high-resolution views of the Sun.

Total Solar Eclipse 2017

NASA's archive mapping the path, physics, and citizen science observations of the Great american Eclipse.

High altitude Observatory

NCaR's division studying the Sun-Earth system, climate trends, and magnetohydrodynamic dynamo runs.

Research Repositories & Datasets

Solar Dynamo Dataverse ↗

Harvard Dataverse collection hosting simulated far-side magnetic maps, active region models, and solar rotation databases.

Kodaikanal CaK Archive (KoSOCaK) ↗

Dr. Jha's package hosting Calcium K-line (CaK) chromospheric data series from the Kodaikanal Solar Observatory.

General Space & astronomy

astronomy Picture of the Day

NASA's daily feature showcasing the wonders of cosmos, complete with brief physicist explanations.

Cassini: Grand Finale

The legacy of NASA JPL's historic Saturn orbiter mapping rings, moons, and final dives.

Juno Mission

NASA's mission exploring Jupiter's atmosphere, interior structure, and auroral magnetic currents.

Hubble Space Telescope

The historic orbiter capturing high-resolution deep-space galaxies, nebulae, and stellar nurseries.
