Dynamo Modeling

Advective Flux Transport (AFT)

AFT is a state-of-the-art Surface Flux Transport (SFT) model that simulates how surface currents redistribute magnetic flux with high physical realism.

While older transport models parameterized convection with a simple diffusion constant, AFT explicitly simulates the convective velocity field utilizing vector spherical harmonics. By capturing observed flows directly, AFT tracks the emergence, shredding, and migration of active region magnetic flux from the belts to the solar poles.

Polar Field Predictions

During solar maximum, the previous cycle's polar fields cancel, and a new poloidal field of opposite polarity grows. By solar minimum, this field serves as the seed establishing the next cycle's amplitude.

We demonstrated AFT's capabilities by simulating Solar Cycle 23's polar field reversal. Initiated 3 years prior, the model predicted the exact timing of the reversal to within 4 months, with 80% of realizations accurate to within a single month.

Simulations of Cycle 24's axial dipole moment reversal and subsequent buildup likewise show close agreement with SDO/HMI observations, capturing plateaus and sudden rises in polarity buildup.

active Region Simulations

active Region 11158 Evolution: EUV 304 Å proxy flux (left) matches the unsigned magnetic flux in AFT (red and orange curves) during far-side disk rotations (grey zones denote Earth-facing HMI observations).

AFT tracks individual active region evolution in detail. By utilizing the EUV 304 Å flux-luminosity relation, we compared estimated unsigned magnetic flux directly to AFT's simulated magnetic profiles.

When an active region rotates to the far-side of the Sun (out of view of Earth-based magnetograms), AFT continues to accurately model its dispersion and transport.

The model allows for two modes of source input:

  • Manual Insertion: Inserting bipolar active regions from databases (like NOaa) to run hypothetical models or predictions.
  • Data assimilation: Directly assimilating magnetograms from satellites (like SDO/HMI), yielding highly accurate maps of the entire Sun (our Baseline dataset).
